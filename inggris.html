How an Old Keyboard Is Transformed into a Pixel Art Wall Decoration
Paragraph 1 - General Statement
Electronic waste is an environmental issue stemming from rapid technological advancement. Old computer keyboards are often discarded when they break or are no longer needed; however, they contain materials that can still be repurposed. One creative way to manage this waste is by transforming old keyboard keys into unique pixel-art wall decorations—such as a heart shape—which offer high aesthetic value while supporting eco-friendly recycling efforts.
Paragraph 2 - The Transformation Process
The creation process begins with preparing the necessary materials and tools, which include used computer keyboard keys, a canvas backing board, a strong adhesive (such as hot glue), a ruler, and a pencil. The transformation process starts by thoroughly cleaning the dust off the used keyboard keys using a cleaning cloth. Next, draw guide lines to ensure the keys are aligned straight and in the correct order.
Paragraph 3 - The Formation of a New Product
Next, prepare a canvas and a hot glue gun. Arrange the cleaned keyboard keys on the canvas, following a specific heart-shaped pattern. The keys remain unaltered, creating an engaging 3D-textured visual effect. Once all the keys are neatly arranged according to the pattern, secure them firmly in place with glue. Afterward, decorate the background of this "pixel art" using acrylic paint—perhaps depicting a sunset at the beach—and finally, clean away any residual glue during the finishing stage.
Paragraph 4 - The Result and Its Impact
As a result, an unused computer keyboard is transformed into a creative piece of pixel-art wall decor that offers both functional and aesthetic appeal. With a total production cost of IDR 30,000 for materials and a selling price of IDR 30,000 (or, alternatively, IDR 45,000), the product yields a profit of IDR 15,000 per unit. Furthermore, this transformation reduces electronic waste and encourages the repurposing of existing materials, thereby supporting a more sustainable environment.

Muhammad Alva Naja Izzani

General Statement
Over time, waste and trash continue to accumulate—a phenomenon that can occur anywhere, including in computer labs. For this reason, we should repurpose the waste found in such environments. Take keyboards, for example: we can reuse old, discarded keyboards by transforming them into something useful—such as the pixel art wall decor we offer. Our product is crafted from repurposed keyboard keys, which are cleaned and arranged to form specific images or designs.

The Transformation Process
The process of creating this pixel art wall decoration primarily involves the use of old computer keyboards. These keyboards become valuable materials for wall art because their individual keys can be reused as decorative elements. The transformation begins with the keyboards obtained from places such as computer laboratories. The keyboards consist of many small keys that can be separated from their main structure. After being separated, the keys undergo a cleaning process to remove dirt and dust. This process ensures that the keys are clean and ready to be used as materials for the pixel art design.

The Formation of a New Product
After the cleaning process, the keyboard keys are ready to be transformed into a pixel art wall decoration. The keys are arranged on a prepared board according to a specific pattern or image design. Each key represents a small pixel that forms part of the overall picture. The arrangement of letters, numbers, and symbols creates a unique visual pattern that resembles digital pixel art. The keys are placed close to one another to form a clear and organized image. Once the desired pattern is completed, the keys are attached firmly to the board using adhesive. This process turns separate keyboard components into a single piece of decorative artwork.

The Result and Its Impact
As a result, an unused computer keyboard is transformed into a unique and creative pixel art wall decoration. The finished product has aesthetic value because it combines different keyboard keys to create an interesting visual design. It can be displayed on a wall as an artistic decoration, making a room look more attractive and giving it a distinctive appearance. Besides its decorative function, this project also demonstrates how unused electronic components can still have value when they are used creatively. By reusing old keyboard keys instead of throwing them away, this project helps reduce electronic waste and promotes environmental awareness. Furthermore, it encourages people to think creatively and discover new ways to turn unused materials into useful and meaningful artwork.

Hanifatul CHasanah

General Overview
Broken, discarded keyboards pile up as electronic waste all around us. Their plastic keycaps take hundreds of years to break down and harm our planet. Yet these thrown‑away pieces can magically turn into gorgeous wall decorations for any room!

The Transformation Process
Take your old unused keyboard and gently pop off every keycap. Wash them thoroughly to remove dust, then leave them to dry completely. While you wait, paint a beautiful gradient sunset scene on your canvas and set it aside for the paint to dry.

Creating New Pieces
Lay the dry keycaps onto your finished sunset canvas, arranging them carefully into a neat heart shape. Glue each keycap into place. The mix of the painted sunset background and recycled keyboard keys creates a breathtaking, eye‑catching work of art. Let the glue fully dry.

Results and Impact
You will get a lovely heart‑shaped wall piece perfect for bedrooms, living rooms or classrooms. Beyond its beauty, this artwork cuts down on electronic waste and reduces pollution. It proves that trash can be transformed into something precious and wonderful
